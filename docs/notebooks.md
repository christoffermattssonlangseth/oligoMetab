# Notebook guide

Two analysis notebooks, mirroring the two complement notebooks in OligoC4b. Both import the gene panel and helpers from `scripts/oligometab.py`; findings are collected in [`metabolism_findings.md`](metabolism_findings.md), the panel in [`gene_panel.md`](gene_panel.md), the datasets in [`public_datasets.md`](public_datasets.md). Sources are in `notebooks/src/` (percent format) and are converted with `scripts/nb_from_py.py`; executed copies with outputs live in `notebooks/analysis/`.

## `analysis_spatial_metabolism.ipynb` — in-house Xenium AD, Xenium EAE, Visium aging, Falcão scRNA-seq

1. **Xenium AD** (347-gene panel). Panel content first: only *Apoe*, *Apod* and *Acsbg1* of the 183 metabolic genes are present, so this section is short: the three genes by cell type, over the TgCRND8 time course, and their co-expression / rank with *C4b* inside oligodendrocytes.
2. **Xenium EAE** (5K panel, 100 metabolic genes, 107 samples). Pathway scores and focus genes by cell type; EAE vs control in every annotated cell type with ≥1,500 cells by pseudobulk per sample (genes and pathway scores); pathway scores along the lesion-distance bins for oligodendrocyte-lineage, myeloid and astrocyte populations and, per pathway, for every cell type relative to its own far-from-lesion value; co-expression of every metabolic gene with *C4b* inside DA oligodendrocytes with the rank among the ~5,000 panel genes and a pathway-level rank-enrichment test; DA vs homeostatic oligodendrocytes and *C4b*-high vs *C4b*-negative cells, paired per sample; spatial neighbourhood tests (30 µm) asking whether *Hcar2*⁺ (ketone receptor), *Slc16a3*⁺ (MCT4) astrocytes, *Hk2*⁺, *Pdk1*⁺, *Plin2*⁺ and *Slc2a1*⁺ cells are enriched around *C4b*-high oligodendrocytes, with the lesion-distance-matched and non-lesion controls from OligoC4b; the mean pathway score of the non-oligodendrocyte neighbours of *C4b*-high vs *C4b*-negative oligodendrocytes; spatial maps.
3. **Visium aging** (whole transcriptome, 6 sections). Focus genes and pathway scores by age group; linear age trend per section for every panel gene and pathway, in all spots and in white-matter-rich spots; correlation with *C4b* across spots, genome-wide rank and pathway enrichment; pathway scores in *C4b*-high vs *C4b*-negative white-matter spots; maps.
4. **Falcão 2018** (sorted single cells). Pathway scores across the lineage clusters (OPC → COP → NFOL → MOL, EAE-MOL clusters, microglia); EAE vs control MOL clusters gene- and pathway-wise; *C4b* co-expression, rank and pathway enrichment in MOL. Cells are the replicates here, so p-values are descriptive.
5. Cross-dataset availability table by pathway, then the written interpretation.

Summary tables are written to `results/` (`xenium_eae_*.csv`, `visium_aging_*.csv`, `falcao_*.csv`, `inhouse_panel_availability.csv`).

## `analysis_public_datasets_metabolism.ipynb` — the sixteen public datasets

One pass per dataset (loaded one at a time): panel-gene availability; pathway scores for every cell; detection of the focus genes and mean pathway score by coarse cell type; pseudobulk disease / age / demyelination contrasts for every coarse cell type (oligodendrocytes, OPCs, microglia, astrocytes, neurons, endothelial, vascular, immune, ependymal), gene-wise (log2FC, Mann–Whitney across samples) and pathway-wise (score difference); inside oligodendrocytes of the mouse datasets, the co-expression of every metabolic gene with *C4b*, its genome-wide rank, the pathway-level rank enrichment and the pathway scores of *C4b*-high vs *C4b*-negative cells with a per-sample paired test; by-condition tables (lesion type, Braak stage) for the human datasets; spot-level means, pathway scores, *C4b* correlation and maps for the spatial datasets. Cross-dataset sections then show: pathway score by cell type averaged over the mouse datasets; detection heatmaps; pathway-score and gene-level fold-change heatmaps per contrast; consistency tables per cell type (how many contrasts move each gene up or down); human by-condition heatmaps; spatial tables; then, as one axis, the *C4b* correlation, pathway-enrichment and *C4b*-high vs *C4b*-negative heatmaps; and a summary. Tables go to `results/public_*.csv`.

## `analysis_ketone_metabolism.ipynb` — ketone-body metabolism in depth

Takes the ketone arm apart gene by gene (`oligometab.KETONE`: ketogenesis *Hmgcs2* / *Hmgcl* / *Hmgcll1* / *Bdh1* / *Bdh2*; ketolysis *Oxct1* / *Acat1* / *Bdh1*; acetate *Acss1* / *Acss2*; transport *Slc16a1* / *Slc16a7* / *Slc16a3* / *Slc16a6* / *Slc5a8* / *Bsg*; receptors *Hcar2* / *Ffar3* / *Ffar2*; fatty-acid supply and regulators *Cpt1a* / *Cpt2* / *Acadm* / *Hadha* / *Acaa2* / *Ppara* / *Ppargc1a* / *Fgf21*) across Xenium EAE (14 of the 26 genes on the panel), Visium aging, Falcão sorted cells and the sixteen public datasets. Per dataset: detection and mean by cell type, *Oxct1* + *Bdh1* co-detection (complete ketolytic machinery), ketogenesis / ketolysis / fatty-acid-supply scores, pseudobulk contrasts for every cell type, *Hmgcs2* coupling to *Cpt1a* / *Acadm* / *Ppara* inside astrocytes, *C4b* co-expression inside oligodendrocytes, by-condition tables for human data, lesion-distance and DA-vs-homeostatic comparisons in EAE, age trends and maps in Visium, lineage-stage profiles in Falcão. Cross-dataset sections: who makes / uses / transports / senses ketone bodies (detection averaged separately over mouse whole cells, mouse nuclei and human nuclei), disease heatmaps per cell type, direction-consistency tables, astrocyte coupling, the *C4b* axis, human conditions. Tables go to `results/ketone_*.csv`.

## `analysis_xenium_eae_ketone.ipynb` — ketone handling in Xenium EAE with the genes the 5K panel carries

The collaborators' central question, asked of the in-situ data alone. The 5K panel has no committed ketone enzyme, so the notebook works with *Bdh1* (bidirectional), the transporters MCT1 / MCT4 / SMCT2 (*Slc16a1*, *Slc16a3*, *Slc5a12*), the sensors *Hcar2*, *Ffar3*, *Ffar2*, *Nlrp3*, the β-oxidation supply chain (*Cpt1a*, *Cpt1c*, *Cpt2*, *Slc25a20*, *Hadhb*, *Hadh*, *Echs1*, *Acaa2*), the PPARα / FGF21 regulators (*Ppara*, *Ppargc1a*, *Pdk4*, *Fgf21*, *Klb*) and acetate activation (*Acss1*, *Acss2*), in all 27 cell types and 107 samples. Sections: who expresses what (all samples and control-only baseline); EAE vs control per cell type, by model (RR / chronic) and by disease course, and per-sample correlation with lesion burden; lesion distance per cell type and gene; DA vs homeostatic oligodendrocytes and astrocytes, *C4b*-high vs *C4b*-negative, NFOL vs mature (paired within sample); what *Bdh1* keeps company with inside each cell type (β-oxidation and *Ppara* = ketogenic side, MCT1 / acetate = export side) in control and EAE; myeloid *Hcar2* / *Nlrp3* detection, co-detection, EAE effect and lesion gradient; spatial neighbourhoods (*Bdh1*⁺ / *Cpt1a*⁺ / MCT4⁺ astrocytes, *Hcar2*⁺ / *Nlrp3*⁺ myeloid cells, MCT1⁺ endothelium around DA and *C4b*-high oligodendrocytes, with lesion-distance matching); maps. Tables go to `results/eae5k_ketone_*.csv`.

## Statistics used

- **Pseudobulk**: mean log-expression (or mean pathway score) per sample × cell type, samples with ≥20 cells of that type; two-sided Mann–Whitney between groups when both have ≥3 samples, otherwise descriptive.
- **Pathway scores**: `scanpy.tl.score_genes` (set mean minus size-matched control set), compared by difference.
- **Co-expression with C4b**: Spearman correlation across cells, Fisher test on detection in *C4b*⁺ vs *C4b*⁻ cells, and the rank of each gene among all detected genes ranked by Spearman correlation with *C4b* (subsampled to 40,000 cells).
- **Pathway rank enrichment**: Mann–Whitney of the pathway genes' Spearman rho against all other ranked genes.
- **Paired comparisons** (DA vs homeostatic, *C4b*-high vs *C4b*-negative, neighbourhood ratios): per-sample means, paired Wilcoxon across samples.
- **Neighbourhoods**: all cells within 30 µm of a source cell (KD-tree per sample); fraction of target cells around *C4b*-high vs *C4b*-negative oligodendrocytes; lesion-distance-matched version stratifies sources by `lesion_distance_bin`.
